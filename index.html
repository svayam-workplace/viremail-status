How this page works

Every five minutes, an automatic check runs on GitHub, away from Viremail's own servers. It loads the website, the sign-in page and the desktop update list, looks up our domain names, and connects to mail sending and mail access without signing in.

Results are saved at least once an hour, and straight away when something changes. If a check fails twice in a row, an incident opens here on its own and closes when the check passes again.
